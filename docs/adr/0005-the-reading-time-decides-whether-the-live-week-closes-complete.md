# The reading time decides whether the Live Week closes Complete

A reading from 22:00 UTC on the Fort Week's Sunday counts as a full week, even though forts destroyed between it and the Monday reset are missed. Without one, the week closes Partial. An earlier Sunday reading also leaves it Partial: calling that week Complete would give a Governor missing from the early ranking a 0 in their Fort Average, while Partial only leaves the week out for them.
