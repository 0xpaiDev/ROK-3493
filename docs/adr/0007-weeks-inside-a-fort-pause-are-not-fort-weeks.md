# Weeks inside a Fort Pause are not Fort Weeks

During a KvK the Council can pause Forts: the reader stops scanning them and Home shows the KvK instead of Fort Rankings. A week fully inside a Fort Pause is not a Fort Week at all, rather than a missed one, because Fort Average counts a missed Complete Week as 0 and two months of KvK would sink every Governor's average. The week a pause starts closes as it stands, Complete or Partial by ADR 0005, and the week Forts resume is an ordinary Live Week.

The pause is a manual Council switch, not tied to a KvK's start or end, because Kingdoms sometimes keep Forts going into the preparation phase. Home follows the switch alone: Fort Rankings while Forts run, the newest KvK while they are paused.
