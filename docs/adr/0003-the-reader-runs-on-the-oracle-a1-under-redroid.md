# The reader runs on the Oracle A1 under Redroid

Fort Counts are exposed only in the game's alliance Rankings, so the reader signs in to the Android game under Redroid and reads that list from screenshots. It runs on the Oracle A1 because the list has to be scanned and updated automatically at night, without anyone's own machine being involved.

## Consequences

The account is automated on an unwritten understanding: Lilith tolerates bot accounts that only read data and bans those that gain from automation, such as gathering resources or gems. Nothing published guarantees this.
